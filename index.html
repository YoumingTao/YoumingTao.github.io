About Me

I am a postdoctoral researcher with the Telecommunication Networks (TKN) Group at the School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science, TU Berlin. I completed my PhD under the supervision of Prof. Dr.-Ing. habil. Falko Dressler and Prof. Xiuzhen (Susan) Cheng, graduating with summa cum laude in 2025. I am also very fortunate to work closely with Prof. Di Wang, who directs the PRADA Lab at KAUST.

I obtained my bachelor's degree (with distinction) in Computer Science from the Taishan (Honors) College at Shandong University in 2021.

Research

My research focuses on distributed AI and trustworthy AI, with a particular interest in edge environments.

Openings: Motivated students interested in research collaboration are welcome to email me their CV. TU Berlin students interested in related topics are also welcome to contact me about bachelor’s/master’s thesis opportunities.
